Problematic Uploads

Missing Reference Coin

If the coin is missing or completely obscured, the AI will fail with a "Reference coin not found" error because it cannot establish the mm/pixel scale.

Extreme Angles

Taking the photo from a sharp side angle distorts the coin into an oval. This ruins the geometric scaling and leads to highly inaccurate width measurements.
