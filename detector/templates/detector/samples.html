Sample Gallery & Rules

Review these 7 critical examples to understand what makes a good upload and what to expect from the AI analysis.

Image Capture Guidelines

Sample 1.jpg

1. Clear & Perpendicular

Capture the image with the camera held perpendicular (90°) to the surface. Ensure the crack is sharp and clearly visible, with no angular distortion.

Sample 2.jpg

2. Proper Distance (Pixel Rule)

Adjust your distance so that the crack width appears greater than 10 pixels. Avoid capturing from too far, as small cracks may not be detected accurately.

Sample 3.jpg

3. Good Lighting

Ensure uniform lighting conditions. Avoid shadows, glare, or overly dark scenes so that crack edges remain clearly distinguishable.

Sample 4.jpg

4. Sharp Focus

Make sure the image is in focus and free from motion blur. Blurry images reduce detection accuracy significantly.

Sample 5.jpg

5. Proper Coin Placement

Place a coin on the same surface near the crack without covering it. This helps the system estimate real-world crack dimensions accurately.

Sample 6.jpg

6. Balanced Framing

Capture both the crack and some surrounding surface. Avoid extreme close-ups or overly zoomed-out images.

Sample 7.jpg

7. No Digital Zoom

Do not use digital zoom as it causes pixel interpolation and blur. Move closer physically to maintain image quality.
