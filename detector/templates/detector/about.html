About CrackAI

Bridging the gap between Advanced Computer Vision and Civil Engineering.

The Project Purpose

The structural integrity of concrete infrastructure is traditionally monitored through manual visual inspections. This process is not only incredibly slow and dangerous but also highly subjective. Two different inspectors might measure the same crack and record different widths, leading to inconsistent safety protocols.

CrackAI was developed to eliminate human error. By leveraging the YOLOv11 deep learning architecture alongside mathematical skeletonization techniques, this system provides objective, pixel-perfect measurements. By instantly cross-referencing these measurements with the ACI 224R-01 standards, the system turns raw images into actionable safety data in milliseconds.
