Instructions & Warnings

Everything you need to know to achieve highly accurate, ACI-compliant crack measurements.

System Workflow

1. Image Upload

User uploads image containing the crack and reference coin.

2. Calibration

YOLOv11 detects the coin to calculate the exact mm/pixel scale.

3. Segmentation

AI segments the crack at the pixel level and generates a skeleton mask.

4. Analysis

System calculates max width and outputs ACI 224R-01 severity.

Image Capture Rules

Must Do

  • Include the Reference Object: You MUST place a standard coin (default 18.5mm) flat against the surface next to the crack. Without it, scaling will fail.
  • Perpendicular Angle: Hold your camera perfectly parallel to the wall/surface (90 degrees). Angles introduce perspective distortion.
  • Good Lighting: Ensure the crack is well-lit and clearly visible without harsh shadows masking the edges.

Avoid

  • Extreme Angles: Do not take photos from the side or bottom. This will warp the coin's dimensions and ruin the mm/pixel calculation.
  • Blurry Images: Out-of-focus images prevent the AI from accurately tracing the precise edges of the concrete crack.
  • Obstructed Coins: Do not cover the reference coin with your fingers or place it too far into deep crevices.

ACI 224R-01 Guidelines

American Concrete Institute
Crack Width (mm) Classification
< 0.10 mm Hairline crack
0.10 – 0.30 mm Fine / Minor
0.30 – 0.50 mm Moderate
> 0.50 mm Severe
* Note: This classification is based on general research practice and interpreted from ACI 224R-01 guidelines.
