Instructions & Guidelines

Everything you need to know to capture optimal images and achieve highly accurate, ACI-compliant crack measurements.

System Workflow

1. Image Upload

User uploads image containing the crack and reference coin.

2. Calibration

YOLOv11 detects the coin to calculate the exact mm/pixel scale.

3. Segmentation

AI segments the crack at the pixel level and generates a skeleton mask.

4. Analysis

System calculates max width and outputs ACI 224R-01 severity.

Image Capture Rules for Dataset & Model

01. Lighting Conditions

  • Use uniform and sufficient lighting (prefer natural daylight).
  • Avoid: Overexposure, underexposure, and strong shadows across cracks.
  • Ensure crack edges are clearly visible.

02. Resolution & Quality

  • Capture at high resolution (preferably ≥1024×1024).
  • Images must be sharp and properly focused.
  • Avoid excessive JPEG compression.

03. Camera Orientation

  • Maintain perpendicular (90°) camera angle to the surface.
  • Avoid tilted/oblique views unless intentionally adding variation.
  • Ensures geometric accuracy & reduces distortion.

04. Distance & Framing

  • Adjust distance so the crack is clearly visible and context is preserved.
  • Avoid Too Far: Crack becomes too small.
  • Avoid Too Close: Complete loss of context.

05. Pixel Constraint (Critical)

  • Crack width MUST be > 10 pixels.
  • Preferred range: 10–25 pixels.
  • Ensures reliable detection and accurate severity classification.

06. Zoom Guidelines

  • Preferred: Move closer or use optical zoom.
  • Avoid: Digital zoom (causes blur & interpolation).
  • Do not over-zoom. Maintain structural context.

07. Coin as Reference Object (Calibration)

Placement & Visibility

  • Place on same surface plane as crack
  • Close to crack but not covering it
  • Must be fully visible & flat (not tilted)

Consistency & Pixels

  • Use same coin type throughout dataset
  • Coin diameter ≈ 80–120 pixels
  • Crack width must remain > 10 pixels

08. Background & Noise

  • Minimize irrelevant objects in frame.
  • Avoid: Shadows of people/tools and patterns resembling cracks.
  • Keep focus strictly on structural surface.

09. Dataset Diversity

  • Vary surfaces (concrete, asphalt) and types (transverse, etc).
  • Mix conditions (dry/wet, clean/dusty).
  • Capture different locations and structures.

10. Dataset Consistency

  • Maintain similar aspect ratios and consistent capture styles.
  • Avoid: Large variations in zoom levels and scale across the dataset.

11. Annotation Friendly

  • Cracks must be clearly distinguishable and not occluded.
  • Ensure boundaries are highly visible for accurate labeling.

12. Avoid Redundancy

  • Do not capture completely identical images.
  • Instead, include slight variations in angle, lighting, and position.

13. Severity Support

  • Ensure dataset balance across all severity levels.
  • Capture widths: Minor (>10px), Moderate, and Severe (wider).

14. Capture Stability

  • Keep hands steady or use a tripod to avoid motion blur.
  • Ensure proper focus, especially when positioned close to the surface.

ACI 224R-01 Guidelines

American Concrete Institute
Crack Width (mm) Classification
< 0.10 mm Hairline crack
0.10 – 0.30 mm Fine / Minor
0.30 – 0.50 mm Moderate
> 0.50 mm Severe
* Note: This classification is based on general research practice and interpreted from ACI 224R-01 guidelines.
